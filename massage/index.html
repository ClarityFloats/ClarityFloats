Massage at Clarity Float Spa

Our licensed massage therapists tailor every session to you, whether you want a gentle, relaxing Swedish massage, focused deep tissue work on a tight back and shoulders, or sports massage to recover from training.

A growing body of research supports massage for reducing stress, easing muscle tension and pain, improving range of motion and helping you sleep better. Pair your massage with a float in one of our Dream Pods or the Dream Cabin, or with our infrared sauna, for a complete reset, just off Providence Road near downtown Columbia and Mizzou.

Types of massage we offer

Every massage is priced by time, not by type. Book 60 or 90 minutes and tell your therapist what you need.

Swedish massage

Long, flowing strokes and gentle kneading to relax the whole body, ease everyday tension and calm the nervous system. It's the classic full-body massage and a great place to start if you're new to massage.

Deep tissue massage

Slower, firmer pressure aimed at the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. Good for chronic tightness and stubborn knots in the neck, shoulders, back and hips. Your therapist will check in on pressure throughout.

Sports massage

Focused work for active people: runners, lifters, climbers, weekend warriors and student athletes. Your therapist targets the muscles you use most to support recovery, mobility and range of motion between training days.

Gentle relaxation massage

Light, soothing pressure for anyone who wants to unwind without intensity. A good fit if you're sensitive to firm pressure or simply want to de-stress. Pair it with a float for the deepest relaxation we offer.

Prenatal massage

Massage for expecting mothers, with comfortable positioning and pressure adapted to pregnancy. Let us know you're pregnant when you book, and check with your healthcare provider if you have any questions about massage during your pregnancy.

Massage + Float

Float before your massage to loosen up, or after to let it sink in. It's the wellness combo our regulars swear by, and our favorite way to experience Clarity.
